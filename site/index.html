Why AI matters in communications and intelligence

A coding-evaluation project built on public sources. The page explains the stakes; the repository lets you test whether code, and the people or models writing it, can handle them.

Three reasons it matters

Volume

Open-source material outruns human reading capacity. The IC OSINT Strategy 2024-2026 calls OSINT the "INT of first resort."

Threat

Generative models scale influence operations. OpenAI publishes takedown case studies of such activity.

Trust

Analysts and communicators must separate documented records from interpretation. Every item in the toolkit carries a source URL and date.
